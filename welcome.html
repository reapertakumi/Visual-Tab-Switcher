Switch tabs with Alt+Q

Hold Alt and tap Q to see your recent tabs. While holding Alt, tap Q again to move to the next one. Let go of Alt to switch.

Tab previews

Each tab's preview is taken when you visit it and kept only on your computer. Tabs you haven't opened since starting the browser don't have one yet: you can capture them all now.
